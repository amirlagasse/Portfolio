AI Text Platform @ Chromie Health

I created a text based shift swap system powered by AI that replaces manual communication between nurses and managers. I built the backend tools for parsing messages, handling approvals, and recording swap history. I also developed a secure dashboard for managers to view schedules and pending requests. The final system made shift changes faster, clearer, and easier to manage.

Nurse 1 requests a swap

Nurse 2 responds, accepts, and posts multiple shifts

Nurse 1 accepts Nurse 2's shift & request is sent to the Manager

Manager approves request

History of swap and calendar updated accordingly
