Scissor Jack Machining Project

I fully fabricated a proof-of-concept scissor jack from raw stock materials, including aluminum, acrylic, Delrin, brass, and PLA. Gained full experience using the Tormach mill, lathe, laser cutter, drill press, and belt sander. I also generated CAM toolpaths using SolidWorks and the Tormach software, set coordinate systems, performed edge finding, and ran conversational and G-code operations. Additionally, I designed and 3D-printed the top and bottom PLA blocks and modified several other components to improve visibility of the internal mechanics while maintaining full functionality. The final assembly shows a smooth motion, accurate fits, and proper tolerancing across all materials.
